HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Each spin fills a 5×5 grid with tile pieces. Adjacent tile borders that form completely enclosed regions award a payout based on how many cells are enclosed.

Tiles

Tiles are either blank or display border segments on two sides. When neighbouring tiles' borders connect and surround a contiguous group of cells, an enclosure is detected and wins are paid.

Enclosure Tiers & Payouts

Win multipliers are applied to your current denomination per enclosure:

Tier Min. Cells Multiplier
Small 1 2×
Medium 3 5×
Large 7 20×
Jackpot 10 100×
Mega Jackpot 14 1000×

Multiple enclosures per spin each award their own payout.
