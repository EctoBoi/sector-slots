HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Each spin fills a 5×5 grid with tile pieces. Adjacent tile borders that form completely enclosed regions award a payout based on how many cells are enclosed.

Tiles

Tiles are either blank or display border segments on two sides. When neighbouring tiles' borders connect and surround a contiguous group of cells, an enclosure is detected and wins are paid.

Enclosure Tiers & Payouts

Couch Cushions

Running low? Search the couch cushions for spare change, it might get you back on your feet!

Loans & Debt

When you can't afford a spin, open the LOAN panel. Each loan adds a fixed amount to your balance and charges compound interest every spin, your debt grows whether you win or not. Repay any amount at any time to reduce interest.

If your total debt hits $3,000 they will give you one chance to repay before the house comes knocking. Later loans carry steeper rates, so borrow carefully.

Clearing your ledger - if you fully repay every outstanding debt, you earn a Debt Clear. Each clear permanently reduces the interest rate on your next loan by 0.5%, down to a minimum of 0.5%/spin.
